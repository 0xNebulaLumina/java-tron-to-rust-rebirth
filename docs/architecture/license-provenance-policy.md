# License and provenance policy

C000.07 establishes the record format and review rules only. No later source, schema, generated file, crate, native/FFI component, parameter, fixture, binary, container, resource, script, or tool is approved merely because this policy exists.

Every introduced or shipped artifact has one record conforming to `license-provenance-v1.schema.json`, bound to exact content digest, origin URL/repository and revision/version, retrieval/generation method and tool identity, declared and independently detected license, copyright notices, modifications, consuming tracker item, platforms/features, transitive/native relationships, and reviewer disposition. Generated code records both generator and all inputs. Parameters and native libraries require authenticated source, integrity signature/digest, redistribution terms, architecture coverage, and runtime loading path. Binaries and containers additionally bind build recipe/base image/package inputs, SBOM, signatures/attestations, redistribution rights, and retention/source-offer location; mutable tags are prohibited. Repository-local authorship is not grounds for a license `not_applicable` disposition: every artifact still requires an affirmative provenance/license finding or remains `review_required`.

Fixtures use exactly one provenance category. `copied` means bytes or expressive content came from an upstream work and records the upstream path, revision, digest, license/notices, local modifications, and required redistribution/source obligations. `mechanically_derived` means a documented deterministic transformation of identified upstream inputs and records every input digest, tool/version/command, output digest, license inheritance analysis, and reproducibility evidence. `clean_room` means no upstream expressive content was copied: records preserve the behavior-only specification and its provenance, separation between specification author and implementer, implementer non-access attestation, independent comparison/review evidence, and any legal direction. `newly_authored` is reserved for independently authored fixtures with recorded author/project ownership and no upstream-derived expressive content; similarity or later upstream input reclassifies the fixture and reopens review.

The pinned `java-tron` gitlink is licensed under LGPL-3.0 as declared by `java-tron/LICENSE`. Any copied or mechanically derived Java material remains subject to an authenticated license analysis before use or distribution. At minimum, distribution must preserve applicable copyright and license notices, include the GNU GPL and LGPL license texts where required, identify modifications, and provide or make available the covered Corresponding Source in the required form. Linking or combining with LGPL-covered code additionally requires review of relinking/reverse-engineering, Corresponding Application Code, installation-information, and source-offer duties for the actual distribution form. A fixture that carries only behavior and is claimed clean-room is not exempt by label: the separation evidence and independent reviewer disposition must establish that no copyrightable Java expression was copied.

Allowed disposition is not a global license allowlist: compatibility is evaluated against intended distribution, linking, modification, notice/source-offer obligations, patents, export constraints, and project policy. Missing/ambiguous terms, `UNLICENSED`, incompatible copyleft/linking obligations, unexplained generated provenance, digest drift, or absent required notices block the consuming item. Legal questions require the authorized legal/provenance approver; engineers cannot waive them. Freeze/UNLICENSED-derived work requires recorded legal guidance and, when directed, a clean-room procedure separating behavior specification, implementer, and reviewer evidence.

Approval applies only to the recorded digest, use, platform/features, distribution form, and consuming item. Re-review is mandatory after content/origin/version/license/generator/input/build flag/linkage/distribution change; new advisory or ownership claim; missing/changed notice; provenance/SBOM mismatch; expanded use/platform; exception expiry; or reviewer/evidence invalidation. Exceptions name the authorized legal/provenance approver, scope, rationale, obligations/controls, immutable evidence, remediation owner/date, and expiry no later than the next release. They cannot override law or unknown ownership.
