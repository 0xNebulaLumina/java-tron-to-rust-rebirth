# License and provenance policy

C000.07 establishes the record format and review rules only. No later source, schema, generated file, crate, native/FFI component, parameter, fixture, binary, container, resource, script, or tool is approved merely because this policy exists.

Every introduced or shipped artifact has one record conforming to `license-provenance-v1.schema.json`, bound to exact content digest, origin URL/repository and revision/version, retrieval/generation method and tool identity, declared and independently detected license, copyright notices, modifications, consuming tracker item, platforms/features, transitive/native relationships, and reviewer disposition. Generated code records both generator and all inputs. Parameters and native libraries require authenticated source, integrity signature/digest, redistribution terms, architecture coverage, and runtime loading path. Binaries and containers additionally bind build recipe/base image/package inputs, SBOM, signatures/attestations, redistribution rights, and retention/source-offer location; mutable tags are prohibited. Repository-local authorship is not grounds for a license `not_applicable` disposition: every artifact still requires an affirmative provenance/license finding or remains `review_required`.

Fixtures use exactly one provenance category. `copied` means bytes or expressive content came from an upstream work and records upstream repository/path/revision/digest, license and notices, local modifications, intended distribution form, and all notice/source obligations. `mechanically_derived` means a documented deterministic transformation of identified upstream inputs and records every input digest, tool/version/command, output digest, license-inheritance analysis, notices, distribution form, and reproducibility evidence. `clean_room` means no upstream expressive content was copied and records behavior-only specification provenance, specification-author/implementer separation, implementer non-access attestation, independent comparison/review evidence, and authorized legal direction where required. `newly_authored` means repository authors created the content without copying or transforming upstream expressive material and records the authoring identity/declaration, exact digest, governing behavior/schema inputs, no-copy basis, and independent authorship/license review. A label alone is never evidence.

The current eight `docs/oracles/fixtures/v1/*.json` files are each classified `newly_authored` in `docs/oracles/manifest.v1.json`. Their evidence chain binds the checked-in `identity.generator` declaration (`c000-fixture-author` version `1`), exact fixture digest, behavior-neutral C000 runner-protocol skeleton, governing oracle fixture schema, and an explicit statement that no `java-tron` expressive content is claimed copied or mechanically derived. Their license disposition remains `review_required` pending independent authorship/license confirmation. The future DR-004 actuator example fixture is not one of these current fixtures and receives no approval until each element has its own complete category evidence.

The pinned `java-tron` gitlink is licensed under LGPL-3.0 as declared by `java-tron/LICENSE`. C000 approves the two actuator-example files only for repository-local inspection and compatibility analysis of their exact bytes. It does not approve copying into fixtures, Rust redistribution, production linkage, or any later implementation. Any copied or mechanically derived Java material remains subject to authenticated license review before use or distribution. At minimum, the actual distribution form must preserve applicable copyright and license notices, include GNU GPL and LGPL license texts where required, identify modifications, and provide or make available the covered Corresponding Source in the required form. Linking or combining with LGPL-covered code additionally requires review of relinking and reverse-engineering rights, Corresponding Application Code, installation information, and source-offer duties. Behavior-only clean-room claims require the complete separation evidence above and independent legal/provenance disposition; they are not inferred from differing syntax or language.

Allowed disposition is not a global license allowlist: compatibility is evaluated against intended distribution, linking, modification, notice/source-offer obligations, patents, export constraints, and project policy. Missing/ambiguous terms, `UNLICENSED`, incompatible copyleft/linking obligations, unexplained generated provenance, digest drift, or absent required notices block the consuming item. Legal questions require the authorized legal/provenance approver; engineers cannot waive them. Freeze/UNLICENSED-derived work requires recorded legal guidance and, when directed, a clean-room procedure separating behavior specification, implementer, and reviewer evidence.

Approval applies only to the recorded digest, use, platform/features, distribution form, and consuming item. Re-review is mandatory after content/origin/version/license/generator/input/build flag/linkage/distribution change; new advisory or ownership claim; missing/changed notice; provenance/SBOM mismatch; expanded use/platform; exception expiry; or reviewer/evidence invalidation. Exceptions name the authorized legal/provenance approver, scope, rationale, obligations/controls, immutable evidence, remediation owner/date, and expiry no later than the next release. They cannot override law or unknown ownership.
