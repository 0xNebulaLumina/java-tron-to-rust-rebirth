# Tracker, evidence, and dependency validation contract

`docs/PORTING_TRACKER.json` is authoritative; `docs/PORTING_CHECKLIST.md` is an exact human projection. Every substantive and `.V` record has a literal, ordered `dependencies[]`. Edges name complete stable item IDs only. The validator rejects missing records, unknown, duplicate, self, and cyclic edges, and `.V` records that do not directly close over every substantive child.

`readiness` is derived, never asserted: a root is `ready`; a non-root is `ready` only when every dependency is `[x]`, each cited evidence record and required review/adoption is current, approved, hash-reachable, unexpired, and unaffected by an invalidation edge. Otherwise it is `blocked`. A record may become `[-]` only from `[ ]` with owner, branch/PR, date, and current evidence. `[x]` additionally requires ready dependencies, passing evidence, closed review, and approved adoptions. `[D]` is legal only for an external blocker with owner, immutable evidence, unblock condition, decision record, and current validity review. Returning to `[ ]` is required when a blocker becomes actionable or evidence/review/adoption becomes stale.

Parent/chunk state is derived from all substantive children and its `.V`: complete only when all are `[x]`; externally blocked only when at least one valid `[D]` exists and none is active; in progress when any child is `[-]` or `[x]`; otherwise not started. Parent prerequisites are metadata and never edges.

Evidence conforms to `evidence-v1.schema.json`, is immutable, and binds the exact command, normalized environment/toolchain, run/case IDs, timestamps, expected and observed result, exit/status, stdout/stderr and artifact hashes, and all source/schema/config/fixture/dependency/toolchain/generator/normalization inputs. Evidence instances live at `docs/governance/evidence/EV-####.json`; tracker and platform `evidence[]` entries are exact `EV-####` identifiers, never prose. The validator resolves and validates every listed identifier; unknown, malformed, stale, failed, duplicate, or wrong-owner references are errors and are never silently filtered. Any input digest change, missing artifact, failure, unapproved skip, expired retention, repository-revision mismatch, or declared invalidation edge makes the instance stale. `skip` is never passing evidence: its concrete owner, reason, unblock condition, and review date govern a deferred/unavailable disposition but cannot complete an item.

Reviews conform to `independent-review-v1.schema.json` and live at `docs/governance/reviews/RV-####.json`. Every review and rerun identifier is resolved without filtering. Pending instances bind the exact revision and artifact digests without implying approval. Approval requires an assigned independent named reviewer, a closure reviewer present in the reviewer set, zero unresolved findings, fix revisions equal to the reviewed revision, current passing post-fix EV reruns, and unexpired retention. C000.V requires separate current, schema-valid architecture, security, and license approvals covering the checked revision.

Dependencies and tools are adoption instances, not globally pre-approved. Each consuming item lists exact inventory identifiers; every instance binds one consuming item, its real local `.V` gate, a current `DD-###`, source/version/features, and security and license dispositions. Unknown, reused, wrong-owner, rejected, pending, retired, unregistered, or digest-drifted instances block effective completion. Inventory-only and repository-input decisions register C000 inputs but do not invent approval for later production dependencies.

Effective completion is stricter than the stored checkbox. A record is effectively complete only when it is `[x]`, every dependency is effectively complete, every evidence and adoption reference is current and owned by that record, and every record-specific predicate passes. C000.08 and C000.09 additionally require fresh, source-revision-bound ownership ledgers whose declared row counts, unique IDs, tracker owners, gates, generator digest, and fail-on-unknown policy recompute correctly. C000.14 additionally requires the complete native platform predicate. C000.V additionally requires all three approved review classes, the ledger/adoption/projection predicates, and C000.14 platform completeness. Readiness and downstream completion are derived from this effective state, not from local checkbox values.

The platform manifest enumerates every initially supported row and separately reserves later-enablement rows without treating them as C000 evidence claims. Coverage totals and result are recomputed from the support scope, rows, current evidence, and reviewed non-applicability; checked-in counters are assertions, not inputs. `F-PRODUCTION-DEFAULT` is the exact set of Cargo features declared by the workspace (currently the empty set), not a synthetic feature named `default`. A passing native cell must resolve current C000.14 evidence binding the exact cell command and working directory, target triple, native host OS/architecture and execution, required toolchains, backend, complete feature set, platform row, and cell identity. A `not_applicable` cell additionally requires current evidence whose review bindings include every approved independent review named by the cell, plus a concrete review scope and reopen condition. Runnable cells without current bound evidence and all deferred, unavailable, cross-built, emulated, unreviewed-not-applicable, or otherwise unrun applicable cells remain incomplete. Reserved later-enablement rows do not satisfy or block C000; promoting one to initial support requires a reviewed manifest change and its full native cell evidence.

`tools/tracker/validate.py` is deliberately dependency-free and never mutates status. It checks tracker structure/graph, `.V` closure, checklist dependency/status round-trip, ownership metadata for C000.10-C000.15, adoption bindings, behavior-manifest zero-gap fields for concrete manifests, recomputed platform coverage, seam ownership, and ownership-ledger integrity. It validates concrete EV/RV instances against their checked-in schema requirements, verifies ownership, revision, artifacts, inputs, retention, approvals and reruns, derives effective completion/readiness, checks governed `[D]` blockers, and compares validation summary fields. Schema documents are definitions and are not scanned as concrete behavior manifests.

The deterministic C000 artifact audit is `tools/platform/run c000-artifacts`. It is a read-only inspection of the workspace: it does not start either node, create evidence, or mutate the tracker/checklist. Its canonical, key-sorted JSON contains one emitted case for each of C000.01-C000.12 and C000.15 plus C000.V; every case reports pass/fail, observations, and the path/SHA-256 identity of each artifact actually inspected. Missing or malformed documents fail their owning case. The ledger cases inspect only the current source-derived `production-ownership.v1.json` and `java-test-ownership.v1.json` contracts, never the superseded legacy ledger snapshots. C000.04 recomputes the oracle manifest's revision, referenced schemas, fixtures, implementations, ledger identities/counts/digests, and regeneration contract; its free-form `status` text is not proof. C000.15 applies the validator's current adoption-instance model, including generated-input digests, consumer/local-gate/decision bindings, source digests, and security/license dispositions.

C000.V is aggregation, not node behavior. In addition to every emitted substantive audit case passing, it internally applies the same current evidence, independent-review, and native-platform predicates used by the tracker validator to C000.13 and C000.14. C000.13 requires current passing evidence owned by C000.13. C000.14 requires current passing evidence with exact command, working-directory, target, native host/execution, toolchain, backend, feature-set, platform-row, and cell bindings, plus recomputed complete coverage for every initially supported platform cell. Reviewed non-applicability requires current bound approval, an empty evidence list, and explicit review scope and reopen conditions. C000.V separately requires exactly one current architecture, security, and license approval class owned by C000.V; approvals bind the checked revision, registered independent closure reviewer, resolved findings and required passing reruns, retention, and exact covered-artifact hashes. Tracker/checklist status fields and checkboxes are never accepted as artifact proof. The command exits zero only when every emitted case and these internal semantic predicates pass, and otherwise exits one while still emitting the complete audit JSON.
